## Debugging

- [konva-devtool](https://github.com/konvajs/konva-devtool) — official browser extension that shows the Konva scene graph and node attributes, instead of the single `<canvas>` element the built-in inspector sees
- [konva-inspector](https://github.com/maitrungduc1410/konva-inspector) — community extension with a profiler and a `$konva` console handle; supports Konva 9 and 10
